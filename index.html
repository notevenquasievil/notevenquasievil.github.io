No2

Not even quasi evil.

No2 is a private assistant that runs on one household's home server. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign up.

What it does:

  1. Reads bills forwarded to its own Gmail account (notevenquasievil@gmail.com).
  2. Saves the bill to the household's own storage with a sensible file name.
  3. Adds a payment reminder to a calendar it created, which the household subscribes to.
