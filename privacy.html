No2 privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026.

No2 is a private app used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  1. Gmail (notevenquasievil@gmail.com only): reads emails, mainly bills forwarded by the household, and adds labels to show which ones have been handled. It does not send or delete email.
  2. Google Calendar: creates and edits events only in calendars that No2 itself created, such as "Bills". It cannot see any other calendar.

What it does with the data

  1. Bills are read on the household's own home server by a model that runs locally. Email content is not sent to any outside AI service.
  2. Bill files are saved to the household's own storage.
  3. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and nobody outside the household sees it.
  4. No2's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
